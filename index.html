Personal Information

Who you are and how employers can reach you.

TipUse a professional email address (firstname.lastname@email.com). Double-check your phone number — most employers call before emailing.

Professional Summary

2–4 sentences about who you are professionally.

TipThe generator writes a draft from your experience, skills and education — then edit it in your own words.

Education

Your degrees and studies, newest first.

TipIf you are a recent graduate, put education before work experience. Include relevant courses or projects if you lack work history.

Work Experience

Jobs, internships, freelance and volunteer work.

TipStart each bullet with a strong verb: built, managed, taught, organized. Add numbers when you can: "Taught English to 30 students", "Built 5 websites".

Skills & Languages

What you can do and which languages you speak.

TipList 6–10 skills most relevant to the job you want — not everything you have ever tried. Put the strongest first.

How to build your CV

  1. Fill in your information step by step using the Next button.
  2. On the Summary step, tap "Generate summary for me" — the site writes a professional draft from your experience, skills and education. Edit it in your own words.
  3. Pick a template and accent color on the Design step.
  4. Tap Download PDF — the file downloads directly to your device.
